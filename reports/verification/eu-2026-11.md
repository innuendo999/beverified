# EU ranking: evidence check, 23 Nov 2026

Rule (owner, 2026-11-23): a provider is scored in `/providers/eu/` when its own documentation or website describes at least one of these:

1. a remote identity proofing certificate: ETSI TS 119 461 or ANSSI PVID;
2. an identity check through a national eID of an EU member state (government or bank eID) or the EU Digital Identity Wallet, live and not marked "coming soon";
3. video identification under BaFin rules (Circular 3/2017 (GW)).

The EU has no single public register of certified verification services, unlike the UK DVS register. Only one certificate below was opened at the issuer's side (Veridas, DEKRA). Everything else is vendor-stated. Plain support for EU identity documents is not enough.

Order: v3.1 score. The ranking shows the top 10 (owner rule for recent rankings). Ties: Persona above ComplyCube (4.0, same order as the UK ranking); at 3.7, a confirmed certificate first (Veridas), then a documented scheme list (iDenfy) over a press release without one (Jumio, 11th).

## Regulatory facts used on the page

- AMLR, Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, applies from 10 July 2027 (EUR-Lex; glossary `eu-aml-regulation`).
- AMLA final report on the draft CDD RTS under Article 28(1) AMLR, 30 Sep 2026: eIDAS electronic identification at substantial or high, including EUDI Wallets, and qualified trust services should be used wherever possible; other remote methods under listed safeguards (news `amla-sends-cdd-standards-to-the-commission`).
- eIDAS 2, Regulation (EU) 2024/1183: Member States provide at least one EUDI Wallet within 24 months of the implementing acts (Commission: by the end of 2026); private relying parties required to use strong user authentication accept it at the user's request within 36 months (glossary `eidas-2`).
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1566 of 29 July 2025 applies ETSI TS 119 461 V2.1.1 to identity verification for qualified certificates and qualified electronic attestations of attributes (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/1566/oj/eng).
- BaFin Circular 3/2017 (GW) on video identification procedures, published 10 Apr 2017, in force 15 Jun 2017 (https://www.bafin.de/ref/19621456).
