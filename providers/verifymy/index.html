Quick verdict: VerifyMy is narrower than most providers in this batch by design: it focuses specifically on age assurance, with a proprietary method that estimates age from just an email address alongside more conventional document and video checks. That focus makes it a strong fit for platforms with age-gating obligations, though it's not a general-purpose identity-verification or AML platform.

What is VerifyMy?

VerifyMy is an age-assurance and content-moderation company founded in 2019 and headquartered in London, led by CEO Ryan Shaw. It launched as VerifyMyAge, focused on age verification and estimation for age-restricted products and services, and has since verified more than 35 million users across social media, gaming, e-commerce, and adult platforms.

Frequently asked questions

How much does VerifyMy cost?

VerifyMy does not publish standard pricing; age-assurance and content-moderation modules are quoted based on platform type and verification volume.

How does VerifyMy's email-based age estimation work?

VerifyMy uses a proprietary method to estimate a user's age from just an email address, avoiding the friction of a document or video capture.

What platforms use VerifyMy?

VerifyMy is deployed across social media, online gaming, e-commerce, and adult platforms, having verified more than 35 million users.

Does VerifyMy do full identity verification?

No. VerifyMy focuses specifically on age assurance and content moderation rather than general-purpose identity verification or AML screening.

What is the best alternative to VerifyMy?

Shufti Pro is the best alternative if you want age verification bundled into a broader IDV/AML platform. Onfido is the closest fit for general-purpose identity verification.

Who are VerifyMy's main competitors?

VerifyMy competes with Shufti Pro, Onfido, and IDnow on age-related checks, differentiating through its proprietary, low-friction email-based estimation method.
