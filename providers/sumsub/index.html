Quick verdict: Sumsub stands out in this category for one simple reason: it publishes its prices. Add a unified KYC, KYB, transaction-monitoring, and fraud-prevention suite that's popular with crypto exchanges and fintechs, and it's the easiest all-in-one platform in this batch to evaluate without a sales call — though minimum monthly commitments can be steep for very small teams.

What is Sumsub?

Sumsub is a KYC/AML verification platform founded in 2015 and headquartered in London, with roughly 595 employees. It serves more than 2,000 clients, including Binance, Bybit, Wirex, and TransferGo, and is particularly strong with crypto and fintech businesses that need document verification, biometric checks, and AML screening in one platform.

product screenshot — dashboard view

Key features

  • •

    Document and biometric verification with liveness and face match — around 99% completion rate in roughly one second.

  • •

    Non-document verification against government databases.

  • •

    Sanctions and PEP screening across OFAC, UN, HMT, EU, and DFAT lists.

  • •

    KYB and UBO (ultimate beneficial owner) verification for business onboarding.

  • •

    Ongoing transaction monitoring for AML compliance.

  • •

    Fraud-prevention tooling layered on top of the core verification flow.

Pricing

Sumsub publishes its pricing: Basic runs $1.35 per verification with a $149/month minimum, aimed at non-regulated fraud and pass-rate use cases. Compliance runs $1.85 per verification with a $299/month minimum, adding AML screening, ongoing monitoring, and proof-of-address checks. Enterprise pricing is custom above that. Both published tiers include a 14-day free trial.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Sumsub cost?

Sumsub's Basic plan is $1.35 per verification with a $149/month minimum; Compliance is $1.85 per verification with a $299/month minimum and adds AML screening and ongoing monitoring. Enterprise pricing is custom. Both published tiers include a 14-day free trial.

Does Sumsub support KYB?

Yes. Sumsub includes KYB and UBO verification for business onboarding alongside its individual identity-verification tools.

Is Sumsub good for crypto businesses?

Yes, Sumsub is widely used by crypto exchanges — including Binance, Bybit, and Wirex — for its combined KYC, AML, and transaction-monitoring capabilities.

How does Sumsub compare to Jumio?

Sumsub publishes its pricing and bundles KYC, KYB, and monitoring in one platform; Jumio has greater enterprise scale but requires a sales call for pricing.

What is the best alternative to Sumsub?

Trulioo is the strongest alternative if non-documentary, database-driven proofing matters more than an all-in-one suite. Jumio is the closest match on enterprise scale.

Who are Sumsub's main competitors?

Sumsub competes with Jumio, Onfido, and Trulioo, differentiating mainly through published pricing and a unified KYC/KYB/monitoring platform.
