Quick verdict: ComplyCube is a smaller, API-first challenger in a category dominated by better-funded platforms, pitching itself on straightforward integration for document verification, biometrics, and AML screening. It's a reasonable fit for mid-market teams that want a developer-friendly onboarding API, though it doesn't have the scale or published pricing transparency of the category's larger players.

What is ComplyCube?

ComplyCube is a KYC and AML technology company headquartered in London, UK, offering an API-first platform for identity verification, biometric authentication, and AML screening. It targets fintechs and regulated businesses that want document verification, liveness checks, and sanctions/PEP screening through a single integration, positioned as a more accessible alternative to enterprise-only IDV platforms.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ComplyCube cost?

ComplyCube does not publish standard pricing; its checks are quoted per volume via sales.

Does ComplyCube support KYB?

Yes. ComplyCube offers business verification (KYB) alongside its individual identity-verification and AML screening tools.

Is ComplyCube good for developers?

Yes, ComplyCube is built API-first, aimed at fast integration for development teams building onboarding flows.

Does ComplyCube do AML screening?

Yes. ComplyCube includes sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media screening alongside its document and biometric verification.

What is the best alternative to ComplyCube?

Sumsub is the best alternative for published pricing and a more established, larger-scale platform. Shufti Pro is the closest fit for broader country and language coverage.

Who are ComplyCube's main competitors?

ComplyCube competes with Sumsub, Shufti Pro, and Onfido, differentiating through its API-first focus on fast developer integration.
