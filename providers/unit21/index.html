Quick verdict: Unit21's newer AI Suite goes further than most fraud/AML tools: its agents execute the investigation lifecycle end-to-end — ingesting alerts, analyzing entity networks, and drafting regulator-ready narratives — rather than just summarizing cases for a human analyst. That ambition is backed by real customers (Chime, Green Dot, Sallie Mae), though pricing is enterprise-only and not published.

What is Unit21?

Unit21 is a fraud-prevention and AML platform founded in 2018 and headquartered in San Francisco, co-founded by Trisha Kothari and Clarence Chio. Originally built as a no-code risk and compliance platform, it has evolved into an agentic AI system trusted by more than 200 institutions — including Chime, Green Dot, and Sallie Mae — to unify fraud detection, AML transaction monitoring, and regulatory filing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Unit21 cost?

Unit21 does not publish pricing; its platform is licensed per capability and quoted based on institution size and volume.

What is Unit21's AI Suite?

Unit21's AI Suite uses agentic AI to execute the full financial-crime investigation lifecycle — from alert ingestion to regulator-ready narrative drafting — rather than just summarizing alerts for analysts.

Who uses Unit21?

Unit21 is trusted by more than 200 institutions, including Chime, Green Dot, Intuit, and Sallie Mae, across banking, fintech, and crypto.

Does Unit21 handle both fraud and AML?

Yes. Unit21 unifies real-time payment fraud detection with AML transaction monitoring, case management, and automated regulatory filing in one platform.

What is the best alternative to Unit21?

ComplyAdvantage is the best alternative if sanctions/PEP data depth matters more than investigation automation. Feedzai is the closest fit for Tier-1 bank-scale payment fraud.

Who are Unit21's main competitors?

Unit21 competes with ComplyAdvantage, Feedzai, and Napier, differentiating through its agentic AI that completes investigations end-to-end.
