Quick verdict: Quantexa's foundation is entity resolution: stitching fragmented, siloed data into a single graph network to surface hidden relationships — the basis for its AML, fraud, and KYC applications rather than transaction-monitoring rules alone. Valued at roughly $2.6B in 2025 and serving tier-1 banks and governments, it's built for enterprise scale, not smaller teams.

What is Quantexa?

Quantexa is a contextual decision-intelligence company founded in 2016 and headquartered in London, UK, co-founded by CEO Vishal Marria, with more than 800 employees and a valuation of approximately $2.6 billion as of 2025. Its platform resolves fragmented data into unified entities and network graphs to power AML, fraud detection, and KYC applications for tier-1 banks, governments, and large enterprises across 70+ countries.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Quantexa cost?

Quantexa does not publish pricing; its platform is licensed to tier-1 institutions based on data scale and deployment scope.

What is entity resolution?

Entity resolution is the process of unifying fragmented data points across systems into single, resolved entities — the foundation of Quantexa's graph-analytics platform.

What is Quantexa's valuation?

Quantexa was valued at approximately $2.6 billion as of 2025.

What use cases does Quantexa support?

Quantexa's Contextual Decision Intelligence platform supports AML/financial-crime detection, fraud detection, KYC, and broader data management.

What is the best alternative to Quantexa?

NICE Actimize is the best alternative for the broadest end-to-end financial-crime suite. Feedzai is the closest fit for Tier-1 payment-fraud scale.

Who are Quantexa's main competitors?

Quantexa competes with NICE Actimize, Feedzai, and Napier, differentiating through its entity-resolution and graph-analytics foundation.
