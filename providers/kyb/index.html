What KYB Actually Verifies

Know Your Business confirms the legal entity behind an account — its registration, its ownership structure, and the humans who ultimately control it. Where eKYC checks an individual against a document, KYB checks a company against a registry, then keeps peeling back ownership layers until it reaches a real person: the Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO). FATF Recommendations 24 and 25 set the global baseline for that beneficial-ownership disclosure.

Core capabilities compliance teams typically require

  • Live pulls from official company registries (Companies House, Sirene, and equivalents), not cached snapshots
  • UBO mapping through layered ownership structures — the "onion peeling" problem
  • Level 1 automation (document checks) vs. Level 2 straight-through processing (STP) that resolves UBOs without a human touch
  • Ongoing registry monitoring for changes in directors or ownership

Where the top providers differ

Veriff ties the onboarding user biometrically to a company's listed directors in real time. KYCAID pairs automation with a Compliance-as-a-Service team for jurisdictions too messy for pure AI. Trulioo covers the broadest set of global business registries in one API.

How this differs from AML and eKYC

KYB verifies the business at onboarding; AML software screens that business's ongoing transactions. eKYC verifies the individual signing up; KYB verifies the entity they're signing up on behalf of. Most banks run all three, often from different vendors.

Regulatory context

Key frameworks: FATF Recommendations 24/25 on beneficial ownership, and the corporate registries — Companies House (UK), Sirene (France), and similar national systems — that KYB providers query directly or through cached feeds.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between KYB and KYC?

KYC verifies an individual person; KYB verifies the legal business behind an account, including its registration status, ownership structure, and Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs).

How long does UBO verification take?

A clean, single-layer ownership structure can resolve in seconds with live registry data. Layered or offshore structures often require manual analyst review that can take hours to days.

What is UBO mapping and why does it matter?

UBO mapping traces ownership through holding companies and trusts to identify the real person who ultimately controls a business, a requirement under FATF Recommendations 24 and 25.

What's the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 KYB automation?

Level 1 automation runs document and registry checks but still routes UBO resolution to a human. Level 2, or straight-through processing (STP), resolves clean ownership structures without a human touch.

Do KYB providers verify company directors in real time?

Some do. A handful of vendors biometrically match the person onboarding a business against that business's listed directors at the moment of signup, rather than relying on a static registry snapshot.

How often should a business's KYB record be refreshed?

Most compliance programs re-check registry and ownership data at least annually, with higher-risk business customers monitored continuously for ownership or director changes.
