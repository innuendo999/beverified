Quick verdict: Veridas built its name on proprietary biometrics — facial and voice recognition that score near the top of independent NIST testing — rather than reselling third-party models. Backed by BBVA since its founding, it's strongest in Spain, Latin America, and Europe, though its funding and global scale trail category leaders built on larger venture rounds.

What is Veridas?

Veridas is an identity-verification and biometrics company founded in 2017 and headquartered in Tajonar, Navarra, Spain, as a joint venture between BBVA and das-Nano. Led by CEO and co-founder Eduardo Azanza, it develops proprietary facial and voice biometric technology alongside document verification, serving banks, gambling operators, and large venues across 25+ countries.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Veridas cost?

Veridas does not publish pricing; its biometric and document-verification modules are licensed per client based on volume and deployment scope.

What makes Veridas different from other IDV providers?

Veridas builds its own facial and voice biometric technology in-house, independently tested by NIST, rather than reselling third-party recognition models.

Who owns Veridas?

Veridas was founded as a joint venture between BBVA and das-Nano in 2017 and remains backed by both, including a €15 million rights issue in 2023.

Does Veridas support voice authentication?

Yes. Voice biometrics is one of Veridas' core proprietary technologies, used by contact centers to replace security questions.

What is the best alternative to Veridas?

Sumsub is the best alternative for published pricing and a broader all-in-one suite. Regula is the closest fit if document-authentication depth matters more than biometrics.

Who are Veridas' main competitors?

Veridas competes with Onfido, Regula, and Sumsub, differentiating through its proprietary, NIST-tested face and voice biometric technology.
