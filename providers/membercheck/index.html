Quick verdict: MemberCheck runs a four-tier PEP screening process alongside sanctions and adverse-media checks, positioned for managed AML workflows with enhanced due diligence and daily ongoing monitoring. It's operated by the same owner as NameScan (Neurocom Group) but targets a different use case: managed compliance programs rather than on-demand self-serve checks.

What is MemberCheck?

MemberCheck is an AML/CTF screening company founded in 2007 and headquartered in Chatswood, New South Wales, Australia, operated by the Neurocom Group — the same owner as NameScan. It screens against thousands of sanctions lists with a four-tier PEP and RCA process plus global adverse-media coverage, serving accounting, casino, financial-services, legal, real-estate, and crypto/VASP clients.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • Four-tier PEP screening depth, including RCA coverage

Cons

  • Headquartered outside the EU, a data-residency consideration for some EU clients (though data is also held in the EU)
  • Smaller brand than enterprise AML platforms
  • Screening-focused rather than a full IDV/lifecycle platform

Frequently asked questions

How much does MemberCheck cost?

MemberCheck offers pay-as-you-go and subscription pricing; a third-party-reported rate is around $90 for 50 scans — verify current pricing directly with MemberCheck.

Is MemberCheck related to NameScan?

Yes. MemberCheck and NameScan are both operated by the Neurocom Group; MemberCheck targets managed AML workflows while NameScan focuses on on-demand self-serve screening.

What is MemberCheck's PEP screening process?

MemberCheck uses a four-tier PEP and RCA (relatives and close associates) screening process alongside sanctions and adverse-media checks.

Does MemberCheck offer ongoing monitoring?

Yes, MemberCheck provides daily ongoing monitoring with alerts for existing customers.

What is the best alternative to MemberCheck?

NameScan is the closest alternative for self-serve, on-demand screening. SmartSearch is a strong fit for UK JMLSG compliance specifically.

Who are MemberCheck's main competitors?

MemberCheck competes with SmartSearch and Sanction Scanner, and shares ownership with sister product NameScan.
