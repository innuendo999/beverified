Quick verdict: Signicat's breadth is its case: 35+ national electronic-ID methods, e-signatures, and KYC/AML onboarding unified in one platform, built through nearly a decade of acquisitions (Idfy, Connectis, Encap Security, Sphonic, and others). As an EU-qualified trust service provider, it's a natural fit for pan-European onboarding, though its per-country breadth is more valuable in Europe than elsewhere.

What is Signicat?

Signicat is a digital-identity and electronic-trust platform founded in 2006 as a spin-off of Kantega, headquartered in Trondheim, Norway, and backed by Nordic Capital since 2019. It unifies identity verification, authentication, electronic signing, and KYC/AML onboarding for more than 1,300 enterprise customers across banking, government, and insurance, built up through acquisitions including Idfy, Connectis, Encap Security, and the UK fraud-and-AML specialist Sphonic.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Signicat cost?

Signicat does not publish pricing; its platform is licensed based on module selection (identity verification, e-signing, KYC/AML) and volume.

What is an EU Qualified Trust Service Provider?

A QTSP is a provider authorized under the EU's eIDAS regulation to issue qualified electronic signatures and trust services; Signicat holds this status.

How many identity methods does Signicat support?

Signicat supports 35+ national electronic-ID (eID) verification methods across Europe, one of the broadest sets in the category.

Does Signicat do AML screening?

Yes, through its 2022 acquisition of the UK-based fraud-and-AML specialist Sphonic, Signicat added financial-crime detection to its identity platform.

What is the best alternative to Signicat?

IDnow is the best alternative for BaFin-compliant video identification in Germany specifically. Sumsub is the closest fit for published pricing and global/crypto coverage.

Who are Signicat's main competitors?

Signicat competes with IDnow, Onfido, and Sumsub, differentiating through the breadth of national eID methods it supports across Europe.
