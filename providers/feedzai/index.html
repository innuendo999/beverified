What is Feedzai?

Feedzai is a payment-fraud detection and transaction-monitoring company founded in 2011 and headquartered in Coimbra, Portugal, with a US headquarters in San Mateo, California, and an office in the UK. Led by CEO Nuno Sebastião alongside co-founders Pedro Bizarro and Paulo Marques, Feedzai became a unicorn in 2021 and, by its own figures, protects more than $8 trillion in transactions annually across roughly 80% of the Fortune 500 and four of the five largest banks in North America.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Feedzai cost?

Feedzai does not publish pricing. Enterprise contracts are quoted through sales, and its cost structure is built for tier-1 banks and card networks rather than early-stage teams.

Is Feedzai good for startups?

Not typically. Feedzai's enterprise-only pricing and implementation model target tier-1 institutions; smaller teams usually look at lighter-weight tools like SEON.

What is RiskOps?

RiskOps is Feedzai's platform combining real-time fraud detection, case management, and risk orchestration into one workflow across the customer lifecycle.

How many banks use Feedzai?

By the company's own figures, Feedzai is deployed at roughly 80% of the Fortune 500 and four of the five largest banks in North America, protecting more than $8 trillion in transactions annually.

What is the best alternative to Feedzai?

SEON is the best alternative for a lighter-weight, faster-to-deploy fraud tool. ComplyAdvantage is the closest fit if sanctions/PEP screening depth matters more than payment-fraud scoring.

Who are Feedzai's main competitors?

Feedzai competes with SEON and ComplyAdvantage on the fraud/AML side, differentiating through its scale and focus on real-time payment fraud for the largest financial institutions.
