What "Mexico-Ready KYC" Really Means

The CNBV supervises banks and fintechs operating in Mexico, and Ley Fintech, the country's Fintech Law, sets specific onboarding and identity-verification requirements for electronic payment institutions and crowdfunding platforms. The market these providers serve is sizeable: Mexico counted 773 fintech startups as of 2024, and its digital-identity-verification market is projected to reach $159.1 million by 2030.

The stacks you'll find here

  • Document parsing for INE voter ID cards, passports, and CURP-linked identification
  • CNBV-aligned onboarding workflows for regulated fintechs
  • Sanctions/PEP screening covering domestic and international lists
  • Straight-through decisioning built to handle high applicant volume across many small fintechs

How this differs from AML

CNBV registration and Ley Fintech onboarding rules cover the identity-check layer; ongoing transaction screening still falls under general AML software obligations, typically handled by a separate system.

Frequently asked questions

Who regulates KYC/AML in Mexico?

The CNBV (Comisión Nacional Bancaria y de Valores) supervises banks and fintechs, and Mexico's Ley Fintech sets specific onboarding and operating requirements for fintech institutions.

How big is Mexico's fintech market?

Mexico counted 773 fintech startups as of 2024, and the country's digital-identity-verification market is projected to reach $159.1 million by 2030.

What is Ley Fintech?

Ley Fintech is Mexico's Fintech Law, which sets licensing and operating requirements — including identity verification obligations — for electronic payment institutions and crowdfunding platforms.

Does CNBV registration cover ongoing AML monitoring?

CNBV registration and Ley Fintech onboarding rules cover the identity-check layer; ongoing transaction screening still falls under general AML obligations, often handled by a separate system.

What documents do Mexican KYC providers accept?

Common accepted documents include the INE voter ID card, passports, and CURP-linked identification.

Is Mexico's KYC market consolidated or fragmented?

It's fragmented across many small and mid-sized fintechs, which is part of why 773 separate fintech companies were operating in the market as of 2024.
