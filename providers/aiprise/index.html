What is AiPrise?

AiPrise is a global KYC/KYB verification company founded by Chaitanya Sarda, US-based (exact headquarters location not consistently disclosed publicly — verify before publish), having raised two funding rounds. Frequently asked questions

How much does AiPrise cost?

AiPrise does not publish pricing; its KYC/KYB platform is quoted based on client volume and requirements.

What is AiPrise's AI Compliance Copilot?

It's an AI tool that automates case review, document analysis, and enhanced due diligence (EDD) report generation.

Does AiPrise support KYB?

Yes, AiPrise combines business KYB verification — registry data, ownership, and social signals — with individual KYC in one API.

How many countries does AiPrise cover?

AiPrise covers 200+ countries, with a particular focus on emerging markets.

What is the best alternative to AiPrise?

IDMERIT is the best alternative for a more established, lower-cost provider. Vespia is the closest fit for deep KYB specialization.

Who are AiPrise's main competitors?

AiPrise competes with IDMERIT, ComplyCube, and Vespia, differentiating through its unified KYC+KYB API and AI Compliance Copilot.
