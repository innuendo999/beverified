Quick verdict: Hawk (formerly HAWK:AI) built one of the earlier explainable-AI approaches to AML transaction monitoring, pairing rules with behavioral AI and a cross-institution pattern-sharing network that creates a network effect against novel crime patterns. Serving Tier-1 banks down to mid-market fintechs, it reports roughly 90% alert accuracy and 50–70% fewer false positives.

What is Hawk AI?

Hawk is an AML transaction-monitoring and screening company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Munich, Germany, by CEO Tobias Schweiger and Wolfgang Berner (formerly branded HAWK:AI). Backed by €51.8M in Series C funding led by One Peak (with earlier backing from Macquarie, Rabobank, and others), it serves 80+ clients globally, from Tier-1 banks to mid-market fintechs including Ecobank, VakifBank International, and Worldline.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Hawk cost?

Hawk does not publish pricing; its transaction-monitoring platform is licensed to financial institutions based on volume and module selection.

What is explainable AI in Hawk's platform?

Hawk's patent-pending Explainable AI produces audit-ready reasoning behind AI-flagged alerts, aimed at satisfying regulator scrutiny.

What is Hawk's pattern-sharing network?

Hawk pools and shares transaction pattern data across client institutions, creating a network effect that helps catch novel fraud and money-laundering patterns faster.

Who uses Hawk?

Hawk serves more than 80 clients globally, from Tier-1 banks to mid-market fintechs, including Ecobank, VakifBank International, and Worldline.

What is the best alternative to Hawk?

Feedzai is the best alternative for Tier-1 bank-scale real-time payment fraud. ComplyAdvantage is the closest fit for sanctions/PEP data depth.

Who are Hawk's main competitors?

Hawk competes with Feedzai, ComplyAdvantage, and NICE Actimize, differentiating through explainable AI and cross-institution pattern sharing.
