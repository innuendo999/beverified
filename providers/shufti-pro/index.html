What is Shufti Pro?

Shufti Pro is an identity-verification and KYC/AML platform founded in 2017 by Victor Fredung and Shahid Hanif, headquartered in London with offices in the US, Poland, and the UAE. It's built around a proprietary AI verification stack — rather than reselling third-party models — and markets itself on the breadth of its geographic, document, and language coverage.

Pricing

Shufti Pro does not publish standard pricing; plans are quoted via sales, though a 7-day free trial is available to test the platform first. Third-party estimates place the effective cost at roughly $0.50–$1.70 per verification — an external estimate, not an official rate, and setup fees apply on some plans.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Shufti Pro cost?

Shufti Pro does not publish standard pricing; plans are quoted via sales. Third-party estimates put the effective cost at roughly $0.50–$1.70 per verification, and a 7-day free trial is available.

Does Shufti Pro support KYB?

Yes. Shufti Pro offers business verification (KYB) alongside its individual identity-verification and AML screening tools.

What certifications does Shufti Pro hold?

Shufti Pro holds ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 certifications and is GDPR compliant.

Can Shufti Pro deploy on-premise?

Yes. Shufti Pro supports cloud, private-cloud, and on-premise deployment, which suits clients with strict data-residency requirements.

What is the best alternative to Shufti Pro?

Sumsub is the strongest alternative if published, transparent pricing matters more than raw country and language coverage.

Who are Shufti Pro's main competitors?

Shufti Pro competes with Sumsub, Jumio, and Onfido, differentiating mainly through geographic/language breadth and flexible deployment options.
