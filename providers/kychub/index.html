Quick verdict: KYC Hub's OpsFlow builder lets compliance teams configure onboarding, monitoring, and escalation without code, unifying identity, screening, and transaction monitoring in one platform rather than stitching together point solutions. Its pay-as-you-go pricing is refreshingly transparent for the category, though its dashboards can strain at very large-bank scale.

What is KYC Hub?

KYC Hub is a financial-crime compliance platform founded in 2018 and headquartered in London, with additional offices in the US, India, and Singapore, co-founded by Jay Rao and Farnoush Mirmoeini. With roughly 102 employees, it serves banks, fintechs, lenders, and crypto businesses through OpsFlow, a low/no-code orchestration builder covering onboarding, screening, monitoring, and case escalation in one platform.

Frequently asked questions

How much does KYC Hub cost?

What is OpsFlow?

OpsFlow is KYC Hub's low/no-code builder for configuring onboarding, monitoring, and escalation workflows without engineering support.

Does KYC Hub cover the full compliance lifecycle?

Yes. KYC Hub unifies identity verification, KYB, AML screening, transaction monitoring, and case management in one platform.
