Quick verdict: Signzy's no-code visual workflow builder is deeply specialized for India — Aadhaar e-KYC, V-CIP video verification, and GSTIN business lookups — while a 2020 Mastercard alliance extends its video-KYC distribution globally. It's the strongest option here for India-specific BFSI compliance, though its depth outside that market requires more configuration.

What is Signzy?

Signzy is a no-code KYC/KYB/AML platform founded in 2015 in Bengaluru, India, by an IIT-trained trio: CEO Ankit Ratan, Arpit Ratan, and Ankur Pandey, having raised more than $38.7M from investors including Kalaari, SAP, and Mastercard. It serves the largest Indian banks, 10 Fortune 30 companies, and a top-3 US acquiring bank, distributing its video-KYC technology globally through a 2020 Mastercard alliance.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Signzy cost?

Signzy's India-market pricing is reported by third parties at roughly ₹5–₹35 per AML/sanctions check plus a ₹2–15 lakh annual platform fee; verify current rates directly with Signzy.

Does Signzy support Aadhaar verification?

Yes. Signzy supports Aadhaar e-KYC and V-CIP (video customer identification process) alongside document OCR and face match.

Does Signzy verify businesses?

Yes, Signzy's KYB tools include GSTIN lookups, MCA director checks, UBO identification, and business address verification.

How does Signzy distribute globally?

Signzy distributes its video-KYC technology globally through a 2020 alliance with Mastercard.

What is the best alternative to Signzy?

IDMERIT is the best alternative for broader, lower-cost multi-country coverage. KYC Hub is the closest fit for no-code AML lifecycle orchestration.

Who are Signzy's main competitors?

Signzy competes with IDMERIT, ComplyCube, and KYC Hub, differentiating through deep India-specific regulatory coverage.
