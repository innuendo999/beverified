Quick verdict: ComplyAdvantage is a sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media screening platform, not a document-verification company — a distinction that matters when comparing it to IDV vendors in this category. Its proprietary financial-crime database and free ComplyLaunch tier for early-stage startups make it a strong AML data layer, typically deployed alongside a separate identity-verification provider rather than instead of one.

What is ComplyAdvantage?

ComplyAdvantage is an AML data and financial-crime intelligence platform founded in 2014 by Charles Delingpole (previously known as Mimiro), headquartered in London. Its Mesh platform serves more than 1,000 enterprise clients with proprietary sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media screening data — it does not perform document or biometric identity verification.

Pricing

ComplyAdvantage's Starter plan begins at $99.99/month for 100 entities, aimed at low-volume screening and monitoring, with a next tier around $119/month and custom Enterprise pricing above that. Setup takes about one business day and plans can be canceled anytime. ComplyLaunch offers free access for qualifying early-stage startups.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ComplyAdvantage cost?

ComplyAdvantage's Starter plan begins at $99.99/month for 100 entities, with a next tier around $119/month and custom Enterprise pricing above that. Setup takes about one business day.

Does ComplyAdvantage verify identity documents?

No. ComplyAdvantage is an AML screening and financial-crime data platform — it does not perform document or biometric identity verification, and is typically used alongside a separate IDV provider.

Is there a free option for startups?

Yes. ComplyLaunch gives qualifying early-stage startups free access to ComplyAdvantage's core screening functionality.

Does ComplyAdvantage do transaction monitoring?

Yes. Real-time transaction monitoring with AI-driven risk scoring is a core part of the Mesh platform, alongside sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media screening.

What is the best alternative to ComplyAdvantage?

Sumsub is the best alternative if you want identity verification and AML screening bundled into one platform. SEON is the closest fit if pre-KYC device and fraud signals matter more than sanctions/PEP data depth.

Who are ComplyAdvantage's main competitors?

ComplyAdvantage competes with SEON and Feedzai on AML/fraud data, and with Sumsub for teams that want combined IDV and AML screening in one platform.
