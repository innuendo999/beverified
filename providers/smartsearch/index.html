Quick verdict: SmartSearch was one of the first electronic AML verification services in the UK and remains squarely focused on JMLSG-compliant checks for legal, property, and financial-services firms. Its individual-plus-business screening in one platform and 24-hour integration are real strengths for UK-regulated firms, though its reach outside the UK is limited.

What is SmartSearch?

SmartSearch is a UK AML and KYC verification company founded in 2011 in West Yorkshire, headquartered in Ilkley, UK, led by CEO Phil Cotter, with a majority stake held by Triple PE. Serving more than 7,000 clients and 60,000 users, it provides JMLSG-compliant individual and business verification for legal, property, and financial-services firms.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SmartSearch cost?

SmartSearch does not publish pricing; its AML/KYC platform is quoted based on client volume and requirements.

What is JMLSG compliance?

JMLSG (Joint Money Laundering Steering Group) provides UK AML guidance; SmartSearch's verification workflows are built to meet these requirements.

Does SmartSearch verify both individuals and businesses?

Yes, SmartSearch offers combined individual and business AML/KYC verification in one platform.

Who owns SmartSearch?

A majority stake in SmartSearch is held by Triple PE (Fund I).

What is the best alternative to SmartSearch?

ComplyAdvantage is the best alternative for broader international sanctions/PEP data. KYC360 is the closest fit for full customer-lifecycle risk management.

Who are SmartSearch's main competitors?

SmartSearch competes with ComplyAdvantage, KYC360, and NameScan, differentiating through deep UK regulatory (JMLSG) specialization.
