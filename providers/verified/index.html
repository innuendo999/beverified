Quick verdict: Verified bundles AML/KYC screening with e-signing and digital identification in one system, built for B2B compliance in the Nordics with a managed-services option pairing consultants with the technology. Backed by Verdane and grown through acquisitions (Pliance, Assently, GRC Watch), it's strongly regional — a good fit inside Scandinavia, less so outside it.

What is Verified?

Verified is a Nordic compliance-technology company headquartered in Oslo, Norway (its precise founding year is not consistently disclosed publicly and should be verified before publishing), backed by Verdane. It serves more than 5,500 B2B clients across the Nordics with AML/KYC screening, e-signing, digital identification, and AI-powered contract management, built up through acquisitions including Pliance, Assently, and GRC Watch.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Verified cost?

What does Verified's platform include?

Verified combines AML/KYC screening, e-signing, e-ID/digital identification, and AI-powered contract management in one system.

Does Verified offer managed services?

Yes, Verified's AML Managed Services pairs compliance consultants with its technology platform.

What companies has Verified acquired?

Verified has acquired Pliance, Assently (e-signing/e-ID), and GRC Watch to build out its compliance suite.
