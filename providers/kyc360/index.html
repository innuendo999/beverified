What is KYC360?

KYC360 is a customer-lifecycle KYC/AML platform founded in 2006 in Jersey (originally RiskScreen), headquartered in Saint Helier, Jersey, and founded by Stephen Platt, a leading AML expert who also founded the International Compliance Association. Acquired by Experian in October 2025, it serves more than 1,400 organizations with Dow Jones-powered sanctions, PEP, watchlist, and adverse-media screening.

Frequently asked questions

How much does KYC360 cost?

KYC360 prices ad-hoc screening by search volume rather than per seat; its full platform is quoted separately via sales.

Who owns KYC360?

KYC360 was acquired by Experian in October 2025 and now operates as an Experian plc subsidiary.

Who founded KYC360?

KYC360 (originally RiskScreen) was founded in 2006 by Stephen Platt, a leading AML expert who also founded the International Compliance Association.

What screening data does KYC360 use?

KYC360 uses Dow Jones data for sanctions, PEP, watchlist, and adverse-media screening.

What is the best alternative to KYC360?

ComplyAdvantage is the best alternative for a larger proprietary financial-crime database. Napier is the closest fit for AI-native transaction monitoring with a sandbox.

Who are KYC360's main competitors?

KYC360 competes with ComplyAdvantage, Napier, and SmartSearch, differentiating through founder AML expertise and its price-by-searches model.
