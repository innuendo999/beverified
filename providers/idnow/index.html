What is IDnow?

IDnow is an identity-verification company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Munich, Germany, led by CEO Andreas Bodczek. It built its reputation on VideoIdent, a BaFin-compliant video identification method required for many regulated onboarding flows in Germany, and has since expanded into automated (AutoIdent) verification and e-signing, extending its European footprint through the 2022 acquisition of French identity-verification company Ariadnext.

Frequently asked questions

How much does IDnow cost?

IDnow does not publish pricing; its VideoIdent and AutoIdent products are licensed on a per-verification, volume-based basis.

What is VideoIdent?

VideoIdent is IDnow's human-assisted video identity-verification method, built to satisfy Germany's BaFin financial-regulator requirements for regulated onboarding.

Does IDnow only serve Germany?

No, though its strongest track record is in the DACH region. Its 2022 acquisition of France's Ariadnext extended its document coverage and reach across Europe.

Does IDnow support e-signing?

Yes. IDnow offers eSigning for qualified electronic signatures alongside its core identity-verification products.

What is the best alternative to IDnow?

Signicat is the best alternative for the broadest set of national eID methods across Europe. Sumsub is the closest fit for published pricing and global/crypto coverage.

Who are IDnow's main competitors?

IDnow competes with Signicat, Onfido, and Sumsub, differentiating through its BaFin-compliant VideoIdent and deep German-market relationships.
