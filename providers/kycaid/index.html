Quick verdict: KYCAID is a strong choice for small and mid-market teams that want a configurable KYC/AML workflow builder without enterprise pricing. It's less deep on watchlist data than category leaders like ComplyAdvantage, but its flexibility and white-label portal make it a practical fit for startups and agencies managing multiple clients.

What is KYCAID?

KYCAID is a KYC/AML verification platform that lets businesses build custom onboarding workflows combining document checks, facial biometrics, and watchlist screening. Founded in 2019 and headquartered in Vilnius, Lithuania, it's used mainly by fintechs, exchanges, and agencies that need a white-label verification portal for their own clients.

Key features

  • •

    Document verification with OCR and authenticity checks across 200+ document types.

  • •

    Facial biometric matching with liveness detection to prevent spoofing.

  • •

    Built-in AML watchlist, PEP, and sanctions screening.

  • •

    No-code workflow builder to configure onboarding steps per use case.

  • •

    White-label client portal for agencies managing multiple end clients.

  • •

    REST API and pre-built SDKs for web and mobile integration.

Pricing

KYCAID pricing starts at $99/month for 500 verifications (about $0.20 per check). A Growth tier at $299/month covers 2,000 verifications (about $0.15 per check), and Enterprise pricing is custom above that volume. All plans bill monthly with no minimum contract term; a 14-day free trial is available on Starter and Growth.

Frequently asked questions

How much does KYCAID cost?

Plans start at $99/month for 500 verifications (about $0.20 per check), scaling to a Growth plan at $299/month for 2,000 verifications (about $0.15 per check), with custom Enterprise pricing above that volume.

Does KYCAID support AML watchlist screening?

Yes. KYCAID includes built-in sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media screening as part of its verification workflows.

Is KYCAID good for startups?

Yes, its pay-as-you-grow pricing and no-code workflow builder make it accessible for startups that don't have dedicated compliance engineering resources.

Can KYCAID be white-labeled?

Yes, KYCAID offers a white-label client portal, which is popular with agencies and platforms onboarding verification on behalf of their own customers.

How does KYCAID compare to Sumsub?

Sumsub offers deeper built-in watchlist coverage and faster average approval times, while KYCAID is generally more affordable for smaller-volume, budget-conscious teams.

What is the best alternative to KYCAID?

Sumsub is the strongest overall alternative if watchlist depth matters more than price. Trulioo is the best budget alternative on a straight per-check basis.

How does KYCAID compare to Jumio?

Jumio has broader brand recognition and more mature fraud-detection models, but KYCAID's workflow builder and white-label portal make it more flexible for smaller teams and agencies.

Who are KYCAID's main competitors?

KYCAID's main competitors are Sumsub, Trulioo, Jumio, and ComplyAdvantage. It differentiates mainly through its no-code workflow builder and white-label agency portal.
