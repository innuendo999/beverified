What "Brazil-Ready KYC" Really Means

Brazil's Central Bank, BACEN, sets customer due diligence rules for regulated financial institutions, while COAF, the financial intelligence unit, receives suspicious-activity reports. A provider built for this market verifies individuals against their CPF and businesses against their CNPJ, and keeps decisioning fast enough to match Pix, Brazil's instant-payment rail, which settles transfers in seconds.

The stacks you'll find here

  • CPF verification for individuals, CNPJ lookup for businesses
  • Document parsing tuned to Brazilian RG and CNH formats
  • Sub-minute decisioning to match Pix-speed account opening
  • Sanctions/PEP screening against domestic and international lists

How this differs from KYB

Verifying a CNPJ registration and its beneficial owners is closer to KYB than individual KYC — several providers on this list also appear on our KYB page for that reason.

Frequently asked questions

Who regulates KYC/AML in Brazil?

BACEN, the Central Bank of Brazil, sets customer due diligence rules for regulated financial institutions, while COAF, the financial intelligence unit, receives suspicious-activity reports.

What's the difference between CPF and CNPJ?

CPF is Brazil's individual taxpayer ID, used to verify people. CNPJ is the equivalent registration number for businesses, used to verify companies during onboarding.

Why does Pix matter for KYC in Brazil?

Pix, Brazil's instant-payment system, settles transfers in seconds, which pushes KYC decisioning toward near-real-time speed since slow onboarding creates friction against instant payment rails.

Do Brazilian KYC providers verify businesses as well as individuals?

Many do, through CNPJ registry lookups, though verifying a business's beneficial owners is closer to KYB than individual KYC and sometimes requires a separate workflow.

Is Brazil's KYC market growing?

Brazil has one of Latin America's most active fintech and digital-banking markets, and demand for automated KYC has grown alongside Pix-driven account opening volume.

Do global KYC vendors support Brazilian document formats?

Leading vendors read Brazilian national ID (RG), CPF, and CNH driver's licenses natively, though document-fraud detection accuracy varies by provider.
