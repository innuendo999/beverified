Quick verdict: Persona's pitch is flexibility: reusable, customizable verification templates that product teams assemble into their own onboarding flows rather than a single fixed workflow. That configurability is a real strength for teams with unusual requirements, though it means a steeper setup curve and less out-of-the-box AML depth than specialized screening vendors.

What is Persona?

Persona is an identity-verification infrastructure company founded in 2018 and headquartered in San Francisco, California, by CEO Rick Song and Charles Yeh. It provides a no-code workflow builder and reusable verification templates combining document, biometric, and database checks, aimed at product teams building custom onboarding and KYC/AML flows.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Persona cost?

Persona does not publish standard pricing; some pay-as-you-go elements are reported by third parties, but rates should be confirmed with sales.

Is Persona no-code?

Yes. Persona offers a no-code workflow builder with reusable, customizable verification templates.

Does Persona support KYB?

Yes, Persona supports business verification (KYB) alongside individual identity verification and KYC/AML checks.

Who founded Persona?

Persona was founded in 2018 by CEO Rick Song and Charles Yeh, headquartered in San Francisco.

What is the best alternative to Persona?

Sumsub is the best alternative for a fixed, published-pricing all-in-one flow. ComplyCube is the closest fit for simpler API-first integration.

Who are Persona's main competitors?

Persona competes with Sumsub, ComplyCube, and Onfido, differentiating through customizable, reusable workflow orchestration.
