In February 2026, Veriff acquired Vespia, with its KYB technology being folded into Veriff's platform and commercial availability expected by mid-2026 — so evaluate it today as a business-verification specialist heading toward a larger combined platform.

What is Vespia?

Vespia is an AI-powered KYB and AML verification company founded in 2021 in Estonia by CEO Julia Ront (formerly Veriff's second employee) and Anton Vedešin. In February 2026, Veriff acquired Vespia; its business-verification technology is being integrated into Veriff's platform, with commercial availability across Veriff expected by mid-2026.
