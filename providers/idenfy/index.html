Quick verdict: iDenfy's pricing model is its headline feature: you pay only for approved verifications, with rejected checks billed at nothing — a genuinely unusual structure in a category dominated by per-attempt or subscription pricing. Add human review of every case and it's a strong fit for cost-conscious fintech, crypto, and gaming teams, even if its brand scale trails category leaders.

What is iDenfy?

iDenfy is an identity-verification company founded in 2017 (originally Identifikaciniai Projektai) and headquartered in Kaunas, Lithuania, co-founded by CEO Domantas Ciulde and Gediminas Ciulde. It serves fintech, crypto, gaming, and marketplace businesses with a three-layer AI and human-reviewed verification process, offering an EU-hosted option and templates for EEA national ID documents.

Frequently asked questions

How does iDenfy's pricing work?

iDenfy charges only for approved verifications — rejected checks aren't billed, which the company reports can save up to 70% versus pay-per-completed-verification pricing. Contact iDenfy for current rates.

Does iDenfy use human review?

Yes, every verification case at iDenfy includes a human review step alongside its AI-driven checks.

Does iDenfy support KYB?

Yes, iDenfy offers business verification (KYB) alongside individual identity checks.

Where is iDenfy based?

iDenfy is headquartered in Kaunas, Lithuania, and offers an EU-hosted option with EEA-specific document templates.

What is the best alternative to iDenfy?

Sumsub is the best alternative for larger scale and published pricing. ComplyCube is the closest fit for a comparably budget-friendly API-first option.

Who are iDenfy's main competitors?

iDenfy competes with Sumsub, ComplyCube, and Shufti Pro, differentiating through its pay-per-approved pricing and human-reviewed process.
