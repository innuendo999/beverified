Quick verdict: Jumio is built for enterprise-scale identity verification — more than a billion verifications processed, Tier-1 bank and marketplace clients, and a KYX platform that extends beyond onboarding into continuous risk monitoring. That scale comes at the cost of pricing transparency: rates are quoted through sales, and third-party estimates put effective per-verification costs meaningfully above budget-tier competitors.

What is Jumio?

Jumio is an identity-verification and KYX ("Know Your Everything") platform founded in 2010, headquartered in Palo Alto/Sunnyvale, California, with additional offices in Vienna, London, Singapore, New York, and Montréal. It has processed more than 1 billion identity verifications and is positioned for large enterprises — banks, marketplaces, and fintechs — that need document and biometric verification alongside AML screening at global scale.

Pricing

Jumio does not publish pricing; contracts are volume-based and quoted through sales. Third-party estimates put the effective cost at roughly $0.90–$2.30 per verification, though this is an external estimate, not Jumio's official rate, and AML screening or premium support typically add to that base cost.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Jumio cost?

Jumio does not publish pricing; it quotes volume-based enterprise contracts through sales. Third-party estimates suggest an effective cost of roughly $0.90–$2.30 per verification, though this is not Jumio's official rate.

Does Jumio include AML screening?

AML watchlist and sanctions screening is available as an add-on module alongside Jumio's core document and biometric verification.

Is Jumio good for startups?

Jumio is built primarily for enterprise-scale deployments; its sales-quoted, volume-based pricing tends to suit large organizations more than early-stage startups.

How does Jumio compare to Onfido?

Both offer broad document coverage and enterprise-grade verification. Onfido has stronger developer SDKs and is now backed by Entrust's security infrastructure, while Jumio has processed a larger cumulative volume of verifications.

What is the best alternative to Jumio?

Sumsub is the strongest alternative for teams that want published pricing and a unified KYC/KYB/monitoring platform. Onfido is the closest match for enterprise-grade document coverage with stronger developer tooling.

Who are Jumio's main competitors?

Jumio competes with Onfido, Sumsub, and Regula, differentiating mainly on its processed-verification scale and enterprise support model.
