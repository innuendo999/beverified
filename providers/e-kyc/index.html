What eKYC Actually Verifies

Electronic KYC confirms that the person opening an account is who they claim to be, using a government-issued document and a live selfie, before any money moves. In the EU, the legal basis usually traces back to eIDAS, the electronic-identification regulation; most eKYC vendors read a document's MRZ line or NFC chip and match it against a biometric selfie in under a minute.

Core capabilities compliance teams typically require

  • Optical and NFC-chip document reading across 190+ country formats
  • Liveness-checked selfie match against the document photo
  • Sub-60-second decisioning for straight-through onboarding
  • Audit trail meeting AMLD6 customer due diligence requirements

How this differs from Identity Verification

eKYC is the one-time gate at account opening. Broader identity verification tools keep checking a user's identity at login, high-risk transactions, and password resets — often reusing the same biometric engine.

Regulatory context

Key references for this category: eIDAS (EU electronic identification), AMLD6 customer due diligence obligations, and the document-authenticity standards border-control agencies use to validate MRZ and chip data.

Frequently asked questions

What is eKYC?

Electronic KYC (eKYC) is the digital process of verifying a new customer's identity using a government-issued document and a live selfie, typically completed in under a minute during account opening.

What documents does eKYC accept?

Most eKYC platforms read passports, national ID cards, and driver's licenses, using either optical character recognition on the printed data or the document's NFC chip where one is present.

How long does eKYC onboarding take?

Straight-through eKYC decisions typically complete in under 60 seconds. Cases flagged for manual review can take longer depending on the provider's escalation process.

Is eKYC the same as KYC?

eKYC is the electronic, remote version of traditional KYC. Where paper-based KYC required an in-person document check, eKYC verifies the same information through a phone or webcam.

Does eKYC replace AML screening?

No. eKYC confirms who someone is; AML software separately screens that person against sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media lists. Most compliance programs run both.

What happens if a document fails an eKYC check?

Failed or low-confidence checks are usually routed to a human reviewer rather than rejected outright, since glare, damaged IDs, or poor lighting can cause false rejections.
