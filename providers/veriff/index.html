Quick verdict: Veriff pairs AI decisioning with human review across 12,000+ document types and 230+ countries — an Estonian unicorn built to catch deepfakes with an iBeta Level 2-certified liveness check. Its published starter tiers ($49–$209/mo) are a rare transparency win in this category, though premium tiers cost more than budget-first competitors.

What is Veriff?

Veriff is an identity-verification company founded in October 2015 and headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia, by CEO Kaarel Kotkas and Janer Gorohhov, with offices added in New York (2019), London (2020), and Barcelona (2021). A unicorn since raising venture funding, it acquired device-intelligence company BrowserID in 2019 and verifies identities across 12,000+ document types in 230+ countries and 48 languages.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Veriff cost?

Veriff publishes starter tiers: Essential at $49/month, Plus at $99/month, and Premium at $209/month, with custom pricing above that for enterprise volume.

Does Veriff detect deepfakes?

Yes. Veriff's passive liveness detection is iBeta Level 2 certified and built to resist deepfake and presentation attacks.

How many countries does Veriff cover?

Veriff verifies documents across 230+ countries and territories, supporting 12,000+ document types and 48 languages.

Who founded Veriff?

Veriff was founded in October 2015 by CEO Kaarel Kotkas and Janer Gorohhov, headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia.

Who are Veriff's main competitors?
