Quick verdict: Fourthline built its reputation inside Europe's regulated financial sector — N26, Trade Republic, Qonto, and Western Union are among its clients — combining identity verification, AML screening, and continuous re-KYC in a single API. It's deliberately focused on financial institutions rather than chasing every vertical, and in 2026 announced plans to merge with Veridas into a combined global identity platform.

What is Fourthline?

Fourthline is a KYC and AML compliance company founded in 2017 (rebranded from Safened in 2019) and headquartered in Amsterdam, co-founded by Krik Gunning and Chris van Straeten. It provides a single-API platform combining identity verification, fraud detection, and AML screening for banks, fintechs, and insurers, serving European clients including N26, Trade Republic, Qonto, and Western Union.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Fourthline cost?

Fourthline does not publish pricing; its modular KYC/AML platform is licensed to regulated institutions based on volume and module selection.

Who uses Fourthline?

Fourthline serves European banks, fintechs, and insurers, including N26, Trade Republic, Qonto, Scalable Capital, and Western Union.

What is re-KYC?

Re-KYC (continuous KYC) is Fourthline's automated process for periodically re-verifying existing customers to stay compliant without manual review.

Is Fourthline merging with another company?

In 2026, Fourthline announced plans to merge with Veridas to form a combined global identity platform spanning Europe, Latin America, and the US.

What is the best alternative to Fourthline?

Sumsub is the best alternative for published pricing and broader global/crypto coverage. IDnow is the closest fit for BaFin-compliant video identification specifically in Germany.

Who are Fourthline's main competitors?

Fourthline competes with Sumsub, Onfido, and IDnow, differentiating through its deep focus on regulated European financial institutions.
