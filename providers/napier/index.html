Quick verdict: Napier AI is a dedicated transaction-monitoring and sanctions-screening platform built for financial institutions and insurers, trusted by more than 150 institutions including Banco do Brasil and Australia Post. Its Continuum sandbox for testing detection models is a genuine differentiator, though like most AML specialists here, it doesn't do identity verification on its own.

What is Napier?

Napier AI is an anti-money-laundering compliance company founded in 2015 and headquartered in London, led by CEO Greg Watson. It provides transaction-monitoring, sanctions- and PEP-screening, and client-risk-assessment tools to more than 150 financial institutions, insurers, and asset managers, including Banco do Brasil and Australia Post.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Napier AI cost?

Napier AI does not publish pricing; its AML platform is licensed to financial institutions based on volume and module selection.

Does Napier AI verify identity documents?

No. Napier AI is a transaction-monitoring and sanctions-screening platform — it does not perform document or biometric identity verification.

What is Napier Continuum?

Napier Continuum is a sandbox environment that lets compliance teams test and tune detection models before deploying them in production.

Who uses Napier AI?

Napier AI is trusted by more than 150 financial institutions, insurers, and asset managers, including Banco do Brasil and Australia Post.

What is the best alternative to Napier AI?

ComplyAdvantage is the best alternative for broader proprietary financial-crime data. Unit21 is the closest fit if agentic, end-to-end investigation automation matters more than screening depth.

Who are Napier AI's main competitors?

Napier AI competes with ComplyAdvantage and Unit21, differentiating through its sandbox-testing approach and multilingual sanctions-matching engine.
