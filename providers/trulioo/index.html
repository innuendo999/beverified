Quick verdict: Trulioo's differentiator is data breadth rather than document scanning alone: its GlobalGateway platform checks identity against government and other databases across 195 countries, covering more than 5 billion people and 700 million business entities. That makes it a strong choice for non-documentary proofing and KYB, though it leans more toward a data-API product than a polished no-code UX.

What is Trulioo?

Trulioo is a global identity-verification and KYB data platform founded in 2011 and headquartered in Vancouver, Canada, having raised approximately $470 million through a Series D round. Its GlobalGateway platform spans 195 countries, covering more than 5 billion people and 700 million business entities across an estimated 450+ underlying data sources.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Trulioo cost?

Trulioo's entry pricing is reported by third parties at around $99/month; this isn't an officially published rate. Full enterprise access to its GlobalGateway data layer is quoted per volume.

Does Trulioo support KYB?

Yes. Trulioo's GlobalGateway platform includes business-entity verification (KYB) covering more than 700 million business entities, alongside individual identity checks.

What makes Trulioo different from document-scanning IDV tools?

Trulioo emphasizes database and data-source checks — an estimated 450+ sources — rather than relying on document scanning alone, which suits non-documentary proofing and high-risk industries.

How many countries does Trulioo cover?

Trulioo's GlobalGateway spans 195 countries, covering more than 5 billion people and 700 million business entities.

What is the best alternative to Trulioo?

Sumsub is the best alternative for an all-in-one platform with published pricing. Onfido is the closest fit if document/biometric SDK depth matters more than database-driven proofing.

Who are Trulioo's main competitors?

Trulioo competes with Sumsub, Onfido, and Jumio, differentiating through the breadth of its identity and business data layer rather than document scanning alone.
