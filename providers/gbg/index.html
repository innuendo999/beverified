Quick verdict: GBG is the rare publicly traded company in this category (LSE:GBG, FTSE 250), built around a genuinely massive data network — 280+ sources across 190 countries — plus a unique location-intelligence layer via its Loqate acquisition. It's less about slick no-code UX and more about deep data infrastructure, grown through a string of acquisitions (IDology, Acuant, Cloudcheck).

What is GBG?

GBG is a publicly traded identity-data and location-intelligence company founded in 1989 and headquartered in Chester, UK, led by CEO Dev Dhiman, with roughly 1,100 employees and revenue of £282.7M in 2025. It combines identity verification, document verification, and location intelligence (via its Loqate product) across a network of 280+ data sources and 190 countries, expanded through acquisitions including IDology, Acuant, and Cloudcheck.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GBG cost?

GBG does not publish pricing; its identity and location-intelligence products are licensed based on volume and module selection.

Is GBG a public company?

Yes. GBG plc is listed on the London Stock Exchange (LSE:GBG) and is a constituent of the FTSE 250.

What is Loqate?

Loqate is GBG's location-intelligence product for address verification, acquired to extend its identity-data platform.

What companies has GBG acquired?

GBG has acquired IDology and Acuant in the US and Cloudcheck in New Zealand, among others, to build out its identity-data network.

What is the best alternative to GBG?

Trulioo is the best alternative for comparable data-layer proofing. Sumsub is the closest fit for published pricing and a simpler unified platform.

Who are GBG's main competitors?

GBG competes with Trulioo, Sumsub, and KYC360, differentiating through its data network scale and unique location-intelligence layer.
