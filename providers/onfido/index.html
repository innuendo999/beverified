Quick verdict: Onfido pairs strong developer tooling with broad document coverage, and since its 2024 acquisition by Entrust, it now sits inside a larger identity-security portfolio. It's a solid enterprise IDV choice for teams that want SDK depth and optional AML screening, though pricing remains sales-only and support responsiveness gets mixed reviews.

What is Onfido?

Onfido is an AI-powered document and biometric identity-verification company founded in 2012 in Oxford, England, by Husayn Kassai, Eamon Jubbawy, and Ruhul Amin, headquartered in London with offices in New York, Albuquerque, Lisbon, and Singapore. Entrust acquired Onfido in April 2024, and it now operates as part of Entrust's broader identity-security portfolio while retaining its own brand.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Onfido cost?

Onfido uses customized, volume-based pricing quoted via sales, with no published rate card; higher-volume contracts qualify for lower per-verification rates.

Who owns Onfido?

Entrust acquired Onfido in April 2024. Onfido continues to operate under its own brand as part of Entrust's identity-security portfolio.

Does Onfido include AML screening?

Yes. Onfido offers watchlist and AML screening — sanctions, PEP, and adverse media — with optional ongoing monitoring, alongside its core document and biometric verification.

How many countries does Onfido cover?

Onfido supports 2,500+ document types across 195 countries.

What is the best alternative to Onfido?

Sumsub is the best alternative for published pricing and a unified KYC/KYB/monitoring suite. Jumio is the closest match on enterprise processed-verification scale.

Who are Onfido's main competitors?

Onfido competes with Jumio, Sumsub, and Trulioo, differentiating through developer-focused SDKs and, since 2024, Entrust's security backing.
