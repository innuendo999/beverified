Quick verdict: Incode's Omni platform handles onboarding and ongoing authentication in one system, trained on more than 400 million identity profiles — among the largest proprietary datasets in the category — and it's been named a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader two years running. Its 2025 acquisition of AuthenticID adds further consolidation, though pricing remains enterprise-only.

What is Incode?

Incode is an identity-verification company founded by CEO Ricardo Amper, headquartered in San Francisco, California, and valued at $1.25B since becoming a unicorn in 2021. With more than 600 employees across 25+ nationalities, it processes 4.1B+ identity checks per year on a proprietary dataset of 400M+ identity profiles, serving Fortune 500 companies, neobanks, and governments across 200+ countries.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Incode cost?

Incode does not publish pricing; its Omni platform is licensed to enterprises based on volume and deployment scope.

What is Incode Omni?

Incode Omni is an end-to-end platform combining identity onboarding and ongoing biometric authentication in one system.

Has Incode made any acquisitions?

Yes. Incode acquired MataMap in 2024 and AuthenticID in 2025 as part of its consolidation strategy.

Is Incode recognized by analysts?

Yes, Incode has been named a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for identity verification in both 2024 and 2025.

What is the best alternative to Incode?

Jumio is the best alternative for enterprise processed-verification scale. AU10TIX is the closest fit for fully automated, consortium-based fraud detection.

Who are Incode's main competitors?

Incode competes with Jumio, AU10TIX, and Onfido, differentiating through its large proprietary dataset and omnichannel single-platform approach.
