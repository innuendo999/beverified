It's self-funded and smaller than venture-backed rivals, which shows up in brand recognition and depth of add-on tooling, but for teams that just need broad, affordable document and data-source checks, the price is hard to beat.

What is IDMERIT?

IDMERIT is an identity-verification and fraud-prevention API provider founded in 2014 and headquartered in Carlsbad, California, by Tony Raval. Unlike most venture-backed competitors in this category, IDMERIT has raised no outside funding, building its business instead on serving banks, fintechs, and e-commerce platforms that need affordable identity checks across a wide footprint of countries and data sources.

Frequently asked questions

How much does IDMERIT cost?

IDMERIT's Basic plan starts at $0.10 per user per month for core identity verification, one of the lowest published rates in this category. AML/KYC and Video KYC modules are priced on higher tiers.

How many countries does IDMERIT cover?

IDMERIT verifies identities across 180+ countries using more than 440 data sources and support for 580+ document types.

Is IDMERIT venture-funded?

No. IDMERIT has not raised outside funding; it has grown as a self-funded, profitable business since its 2014 founding.

Does IDMERIT support AML screening?

Yes. IDMERIT offers AML, KYC, and PEP/OFAC screening alongside its core identity-verification checks.

What is the best alternative to IDMERIT?

Sumsub is the best alternative if you want a more established, published-pricing all-in-one platform. Trulioo is the closest fit for broader data-layer proofing at enterprise scale.

Who are IDMERIT's main competitors?

IDMERIT competes with Trulioo and Sumsub, differentiating mainly on price — its published per-user rate is the lowest in this category.
