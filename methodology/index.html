Our Methodology

We score every KYC and AML provider in this directory against 42 criteria across six weighted categories. No score is assigned by hand without a documented source behind it.

How we collect the data

  1. 1
    Sandbox testing

    Our analysts run real verification flows in each vendor's test environment.

  2. 2
    Regulatory filings review

    Public enforcement actions and licensing records are checked against vendor claims.

  3. 3
    Cross-referencing

    G2, Trustpilot, and Capterra reviews are checked against our own findings.

  4. 4
    Price verification

    A quote is requested from the vendor's sales team and compared to public pricing pages.

A score reflects how a provider performed against our rubric at the time of testing. It is not a warranty and does not replace your own legal or security review.

What this score does not measure

  • −

    We do not load-test any vendor's API beyond a 48-hour sandbox window, so our support scores don't reflect performance at production scale.

  • −

    We do not have access to non-public regulatory examinations, only public enforcement actions and disclosures.

  • −

    A score is not a legal opinion. Selecting a top-ranked provider does not itself satisfy a regulator's expectations for vendor due diligence.

Conflicts of interest & monetization

BeVerified earns a commission when a reader signs up with a provider through a link on this site, and providers can pay to appear in a featured slot on category pages. Paid placement changes where a provider is displayed. It does not change the score above it — that number comes from the rubric on this page, calculated the same way for every vendor whether or not they advertise with us.

Correction policy

If a provider or reader believes a data point is wrong, email corrections@beverified.org with a source. We review disputed line items within 5 business days and update the entry immediately if the claim checks out.

Frequently asked questions

Can a provider pay for the #1 spot?

No. Paid placement can secure a labeled "Featured" or "Sponsored" slot in a category list, but it does not raise a provider's score. Score and placement are calculated and displayed separately.

How often do you update scores?

We re-test every provider at least once per quarter and update pricing data on a rolling 90-day cycle.

Who funds BeVerified?

Affiliate commissions from provider sign-ups and paid featured placements. See the disclosure above for how that is separated from scoring.

Do vendors see their score before publication?

We share draft scores with vendors for factual review only. Vendors can correct factual errors; they cannot request a different opinion score.

What happens if a provider disputes a score?

They can submit corrections with supporting evidence to corrections@beverified.org; we review within 5 business days.

Does a high score guarantee compliance for my business?

No. A score reflects our rubric, not a legal opinion. You should still complete your own due diligence before signing a contract.
