Veriff and Persona both check a government ID and a selfie, screen people against watchlists and sell age checks. They come at it from different ends. Veriff, from Tallinn, sells a ready-made verification flow with a public price list, wide document coverage and a free trial. Persona, from San Francisco, sells building blocks that product teams assemble into their own flows, with business verification, reusable identity and a long list of age check methods on top. Every figure below comes from the same records as our [Veriff review](/providers/veriff/) and [Persona review](/providers/persona/), checked on the date shown in each table.

## Pricing

Veriff charges a monthly minimum plus a fee per verification on each self-serve plan, and sells AML screening and ongoing monitoring as add-ons. Its Enterprise plan, for 5,000 or more verifications a month, is quoted. Persona publishes one number: its Essential plan starts at $250 a month and runs for at least 12 months. Growth, which adds KYB, and Enterprise are quoted. Persona bills per successful verification, so drop-offs caused by user error are not charged. For how both compare with the rest of the market, see the [KYC Pricing Index](/publications/kyc-pricing-index/).

## What each platform covers

The widest gap is outside identity checks. Persona has KYB with UBO verification and business monitoring, reusable identity for returning users, case management and a choice of US or EU data centres. Veriff's KYB sits in a limited access program, and we found no reusable ID product or data region choice. On age checks, Veriff offers the ID document and facial estimation; Persona adds email-based estimation, mobile operator and credit card checks, digital ID claims and a reusable age record.

## Certifications and lab tests

Both liveness products hold an iBeta ISO/IEC 30107-3 Level 2 letter, and both are on the UK register of digital verification services. Veriff also holds FIDO certification for face verification and document authenticity. Persona's government product is FedRAMP Moderate authorized. Each vendor's ISO 27001 and SOC 2 claims are stated by the vendor; we could not open the certificates, so they count for less in the score than a confirmed one.

## Integration and time to launch

Both publish API documentation without a login, ship web, iOS and Android SDKs and offer a hosted or no-code flow. The difference is how you start: Veriff runs a 15-day free trial for up to 50 sessions with no credit card, while Persona has a sandbox mode but we found no self-serve sign-up.

## What to test in a proof of concept

Public evidence cannot tell you how either vendor performs on your users. Run both on the same sample of real traffic from your main markets and ask each vendor for these numbers on that sample:

1. **Completion rate:** the share of genuine users who finish the flow, by device and country. Veriff's Trustpilot reviews from end users mention selfie capture failures, so watch this one on mobile.
2. **False rejection rate:** genuine users who fail, and how many pass on a second try.
3. **Manual review:** how many sessions go to a person and how long a decision takes. Veriff includes human review from its Plus plan; ask Persona what its plans include.
4. **Deepfake and injection attacks:** we found no independent injection attack test for either vendor. Ask for one, or run your own attempts with a virtual camera.
5. **Configuration effort:** Persona's flexibility means more setup. Time how long your team needs to build the flow you want on each.
6. **Total cost at your volume:** for Veriff, the per-check fee, the monthly minimum and any AML add-on; for Persona, the plan fee, the 12-month term and the quote for KYB or monitoring.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Veriff** if you want to see the price before a sales call, start with a free trial, or verify people across many countries and document types. Weigh its 2025 breach and the class actions that followed, and plan a separate vendor if you need KYB with UBO checks.

Choose **Persona** if you want KYB, reusable identity and several age check methods from one vendor, need EU or US data residency, or sell to US government buyers. Plan for a 12-month contract and more configuration work.
