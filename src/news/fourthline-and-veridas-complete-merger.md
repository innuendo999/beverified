Two European identity verification vendors are now one company. Fourthline sells identity checks, AML screening and re-KYC to banks and fintechs, mostly in Europe. Veridas, from Pamplona, builds its own face and voice biometrics and is strongest in Spain and Latin America. Here is what the closing announcement says, what it leaves open, and what customers of either vendor can ask.

## What closed on 3 November

[Fourthline's announcement](https://www.fourthline.com/news/fourthline-and-veridas-complete-merger) says the merger closed on 3 November 2026, "following the satisfaction of all closing conditions". The deal was [first announced on 16 July 2026](https://www.fourthline.com/news/fourthline-and-veridas-join-forces-to-fight-identity-fraud-with-a-global-identity-platform). The announcement does not list the conditions or the price.

The money comes from three firms. Finch Capital, already a Fourthline shareholder, Seaya Andromeda as a new equity investor and Ashgrove Capital as a debt lender provide up to €65m together. The companies say it will pay for product work, acquisitions, refinancing of debt and a reorganisation of shareholdings that gives Veridas shareholders partial liquidity.

## Who runs it

| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| Chief executive | Paul Stoddart, Fourthline CEO |
| President | Eduardo Azanza, Veridas co-founder and CEO |
| Supervisory board chair | Radboud Vlaar, Finch Capital |
| Joining the supervisory board of Fourthline Group Holding B.V. | Esteban Morrás (until now president of Veridas), Philip van Engers (Finch Capital), Alfonso Diaz (Seaya Andromeda) |

The combined company is headquartered in Amsterdam, with offices in Stockholm, Pamplona, Barcelona, Mexico, São Paulo and Boston. No new brand name has been announced.

## What the companies say they bring

These are the companies' own figures, not independently checked:

- more than €50m of combined revenue expected in 2026, and €85m of annual recurring revenue expected in 2029;
- both businesses EBITDA-positive at closing, and about €20m of annual run-rate synergies;
- on track for 200 million verifications in 2026;
- Fourthline active in more than 50 countries, Veridas with more than 350 clients in 25 countries.

The product logic is Fourthline's compliance and decisioning layer on top of Veridas's biometric, document and voice technology. Our reviews of [Fourthline](/providers/fourthline/) and [Veridas](/providers/veridas/) cover each product, its certifications and its pricing as published today.

## What changes for customers now

According to the closing release, existing contracts, integrations and local support continue, customers do not need to take any action, and the platforms will be integrated in phases. Paul Stoddart's first stated priority is continuity of service for existing customers.

Questions worth asking your account manager:

- Which legal entity will hold your contract after the reorganisation, and when?
- Which product will you be moved to, if any, and on what timeline?
- Will data processing locations, sub-processors and certifications stay the same during the integration?
- Does your price, support level or liveness provider change when the platforms are combined?
