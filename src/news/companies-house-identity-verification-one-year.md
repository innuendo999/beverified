Companies House marked the first year of mandatory identity verification with a [progress update on 18 November 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/identity-verification-is-making-the-uks-company-register-more-trustworthy). Verification became a legal requirement under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act on [18 November 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/companies-house-confirms-identity-verification-rollout-from-18-november-2025) and was phased in over 12 months. That phase-in is now over for existing directors. For anyone running [KYB checks](/providers/kyb/) on UK companies, most people on the register are now verified, and Companies House plans to show the gaps on the register itself.

## The numbers Companies House published

The headline is that "around 90% of live companies" have complied and more than 12 million appointments have been verified. The key data in the notes to editors is dated 11 November 2026:

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Companies that met the requirements linked to their confirmation statement | 88.1% |
| Companies on a compliance or enforcement pathway | 11.4% |
| Appointments verified | 12.18 million (85.55% of appointments due, 82.94% of all appointments) |
| Director appointments verified | 7.32 million |
| PSC appointments verified | 4.54 million |
| LLP member appointments verified | 135,000 |

Companies House also says more than 75,000 personal data corrections followed verification activity, and 14,000 address errors for people with significant control (PSCs) were found. In its own perceptions survey, 86% of customers found the process straightforward. Quarterly figures sit in a separate [management information series](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/companies-house-management-information-identity-verification-statistics); the press release figures are a later snapshot and are not the same measure.

## Who had to verify, and by when

Since 18 November 2025, a new company cannot incorporate unless its directors have verified, and a new director cannot be appointed to an existing company without verifying. New PSCs have 14 days to verify.

For people already on the register, the deadline depended on the company:

- existing directors verify alongside the confirmation statement due between 18 November 2025 and 17 November 2026;
- an existing PSC who is also a director verifies within 14 days of the confirmation statement due date;
- an existing PSC who is not a director verifies within 14 days of the start of their birth month.

So the last director deadlines fell on 17 November 2026, while some PSC deadlines still run. Companies House says that by the end of March 2027 every company will either have met the requirements linked to its confirmation statement or be "on an appropriate pathway to compliance or enforcement action".

## Enforcement has started

Companies House has "begun targeted enforcement activity". Its [non-compliance approach](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-approach-to-non-compliance-with-mandatory-identity-verification/companies-house-approach-to-non-compliance-with-mandatory-identity-verification), updated on 19 November 2026, starts with letters and a chance to make representations. After that it names three main routes: prosecution, referral to the Insolvency Service, and financial penalties. The paper states that "it is unlawful for a director to act as a director without completing identity verification", and that if one director is in breach, the whole company is in breach too. It gives no penalty amounts.

Financial penalty warning letters went out from July 2026. The [first Insolvency Service prosecutions](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/directors-warned-to-verify-identities-with-companies-house-following-first-insolvency-service-prosecutions) came on 16 September 2026 at City of London Magistrates' Court, where three directors of two companies were fined. One had verified but was convicted for failing to take reasonable steps to stop an unverified co-director from acting. Separately, the 18 November update counts 131 convictions of directors prosecuted for failing to file confirmation statements, where not verifying was treated as an aggravating factor.

Next, missed checks will show on the register. Companies House says it "will put notes on the public register where identity verification requirements have not been completed by the legal deadline". It did not give a date and says more details will come before the measure starts.

## What it means for KYB teams

A [company registry check](/glossary/company-registry-check/) on a UK business reads this register, so the change reaches every firm that onboards UK companies. Based on what Companies House has published:

- A verified director or PSC means Companies House checked that person's identity. It does not replace your own customer due diligence, and it says nothing about whether the PSC data is complete.
- Unverified directors on a company that should have filed by now are worth a look. Once register notes appear, they are a signal your screening can pick up, much like a late filing.
- The 14,000 PSC address errors found so far are a reminder that [beneficial ownership](/glossary/ultimate-beneficial-owner/) data on the register is still being cleaned. Changes on the register work best as triggers for review, as described in our guide to [perpetual KYB](/guide/perpetual-kyb/).
