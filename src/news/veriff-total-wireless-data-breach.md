Identity verification vendors hold the most sensitive files a company collects: photos of passports and driving licences. When one of them is breached, the customer brand sends the letters, but the questions land on the vendor. That is what happened to Veriff, the Estonian IDV provider, after an intrusion in November 2025. Here is what the public record shows, what Veriff has said and what is still open.

## What happened

Total Wireless, a prepaid mobile brand owned by Verizon, used Veriff to check the identity of customers who signed up for a promotion. Its [notice of data breach](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2026-36-total-wireless/download), dated 9 January 2026, says Veriff told it that "an unauthorized party obtained certain personal information" relating to some Total Wireless customers from Veriff's systems. Veriff gave Total Wireless the list of affected people on 10 December 2025. The complaints filed later put the unauthorised access on or around 18 November 2025.

According to the notice, the data was:

- an image of the government-issued ID the customer gave Veriff;
- for some people, their postal address and date of birth.

Total Wireless says its own systems were not affected. It notified law enforcement, filed notices with state attorneys general including [New Hampshire](https://mm.nh.gov/files/uploads/doj/remote-docs/veriff-20260113.pdf), and offered affected customers a year of Experian IdentityWorks. [CyberInsider](https://cyberinsider.com/verizon-owned-brand-total-wireless-suffers-breach-exposing-customer-data/) reported that the letters went to 8,583 customers.

## What Veriff said

We found no statement about the incident on Veriff's own website. A Veriff spokesperson gave one to CyberInsider in February 2026. In it, Veriff attributes the incident to phishing aimed at its customers: a customer employee's credentials were captured through a phishing site. Veriff said customers who had followed its security recommendations were not affected, and urged them to use single sign-on, IP allowlisting and least-privilege access, and to train staff who use the Veriff portal.

Total Wireless's notice adds that Veriff secured its systems, determined the nature of the issue and hired a cybersecurity firm. We found no statement from either company on whether data of other Veriff clients was accessed.

## The lawsuits

Three proposed class actions name both Veriff OÜ and Total Wireless's operating company, all in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York and filed in January 2026, as listed by [Top Class Actions](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/multiple-class-actions-claim-total-wireless-veriff-failed-to-protect-customer-pii-in-data-breach):

| Case | Number |
|---|---|
| Stockton v. Veriff OU et al. | 1:26-cv-00520 |
| Reed v. Veriff OU et al. | 1:26-cv-00465 |
| McLaughlin v. Veriff OU et al. | 1:26-cv-00566 |

The [McLaughlin complaint](https://truthinadvertising.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/McLaughlin-v-Veriff-and-Verizon-Value-dba-Total-Wireless.pdf) alleges the ID data was stored unencrypted and unredacted. These are allegations, not findings. [OpenClassActions](https://openclassactions.com/lawsuits/data-breaches/total-wireless-veriff-data-breach-class-action-lawsuit.php) reported in July 2026 that the cases were still at the complaint stage, with no class certified and no settlement.

## What has happened since

As of 5 November 2026 we found no regulator action against Veriff over the incident, no settlement and no further disclosure from Veriff. ## What buyers of any IDV vendor can ask

The cause Veriff describes, a stolen portal login, is a risk with every vendor whose dashboard shows ID images. Questions worth asking before you sign or renew:

- Can portal access be limited by SSO, IP allowlist and roles, and are these on by default?
- How long are ID images kept, and can retention be shortened or images redacted after a decision?
- Are document images encrypted at rest, and who at the vendor can see them?
- How fast will the vendor notify you of an incident, and is that in the contract?
