A year ago Experian, the FTSE 100 credit data company, bought KYC360, a small Jersey company that sells AML screening, onboarding and customer lifecycle software to regulated firms. Experian bought it for its UK and Ireland business. Deals like this change who a compliance team is really buying from. Here is what was agreed, what Experian and KYC360 promised customers, and what we could confirm a year later.

## What Experian bought

Experian [announced the deal on 27 October 2025](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/107698/experian-acquires-kyc360). Its [half-year results](https://www.experianplc.com/content/dam/marketing/global/plc/en/assets/documents/results-and-presentations/2025/Experian-FY26-H1-results-announcement.pdf) give the legal detail: on 24 October 2025 the group completed the purchase of the entire share capital of KYC Global Technologies Limited, the Jersey company behind KYC360, with its subsidiaries. The price was not disclosed, as [RegTech Analyst](https://regtechanalyst.com/experian-acquires-kyc360-to-strengthen-fraud-prevention/) also noted. Experian said it would report the goodwill and other values in its 2026 annual report.

Before the sale, KYC360 was majority owned by its founder, Stephen Platt, according to [its company page](https://kyc360.com/company). It grew out of the RiskScreen screening product. Our [KYC360 review](/providers/kyc360/) covers the product, its search-credit pricing for ad-hoc screening and its sources.

## What Experian said it would do

Experian's plan was to fold KYC360's KYC and KYB tools into Ascend, its cloud platform for data, analytics, credit risk and fraud, and to make them available to clients from 2026. Malin Holmberg, CEO of Experian UK and Ireland, said the purchase "significantly boosts our capabilities". For buyers, the pitch is one vendor for credit data, fraud tools and AML workflow.

## What KYC360 told its customers

In a customer note [updated in April 2026](https://kyc360.com/knowledge-hub/resources/news-what-experians-acquisition-of-kyc360-means-for-you), KYC360 said three things:

- Stephen Platt (CEO and founder), Tom Devlin (CCO and co-founder), Neal King (head of technology and co-founder) and the wider leadership team would stay in their roles and guide the integration.
- The move into Ascend was planned over the next year and would not change how customers access or use KYC360.
- The way customers hear from KYC360 would change as the integration went on.

## What has happened since

We checked KYC360's and Experian's public pages on 5 November 2026.

| Item | April 2026 | 5 November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Who leads KYC360 | Stephen Platt, CEO and founder | Tom Devlin, managing director, "within the wider Experian Group"; Platt listed as founder |
| Ascend integration | "Planned over the next year" | No announcement found that it is live |

The [company page](https://kyc360.com/company) no longer names a CEO, and it does not say whether Stephen Platt still has an operating role. Neither company has published a statement on the change that we could find.

## What customers and buyers can ask

None of this means the product has changed. It does mean a buyer should check who they are contracting with and what the roadmap is. Questions worth putting to KYC360 or Experian:

- Which legal entity signs the contract, and does it change when the Ascend integration goes live?
- Will screening data, pricing and the search-credit model stay as they are on the current platform?
- Which Experian data and fraud tools can be added, and are they optional, as KYC360 said in April?
- Who leads the product now, and is the published roadmap still current?
