## Pricing models

### Per check, no minimum

You pay for each check you run. KYCAID, Zyphe and Enigma work this way, and NameScan sells prepaid scan packages with no contract or minimum. This model suits low or uneven volume, and the price list is easy to read.

### Per check with a monthly minimum

The vendor publishes a unit price and a floor. Sumsub, Veriff and iDenfy all publish plans in this shape. Below the floor you pay the floor; above it, each extra check is billed at the unit price.

### Flat plan fee with an allowance

A monthly fee buys a set number of checks, monitored entities or credits. ComplyAdvantage, Sanction Scanner and SEON price their entry plans this way. ComplyCube is a hybrid: the monthly fee converts into credits you spend at its per-check rates.

### Credits

Some vendors sell credits that convert into different checks at different rates. KYC360 counts searches in credits ({{ pricingIndex.price("kyc360", "Credits per search") }}), and Enigma prices its credits at {{ pricingIndex.price("enigma", "Credit price") | replace(", same in API, Console and agents", "") }}. CryptoSwift's Travel Rule plans come with monthly credits, but its pricing page does not say what one credit buys.

### Free tiers and starting credit

Shufti Pro's Free Forever plan allows {{ pricingIndex.price("shufti-pro", "Free Forever") | replace("$0, ", "") }}. KYCAID gives {{ pricingIndex.price("kycaid", "Credit on registration") }} of credit on registration, and Zyphe gives a one-time signup credit of {{ pricingIndex.price("zyphe", "Signup credit") | replace(" one-time", "") | replace(", no expiry", " that does not expire") }}. Sumsub offers a free trial ({{ pricingIndex.price("sumsub", "Free trial") }}). These are for testing and very small volumes.

### Per monthly active user

None of the vendor price lists we track charges per monthly active user. The closest figure is IDMERIT's Capterra listing, quoted per user per month, and it is a third-party listing with an unclear unit for an identity check.

## What drives the price

### Document checks against database checks

Reading and verifying an ID document with a selfie costs more than matching a name and date of birth against a government or credit database. KYCAID's non-document checks against government databases cost {{ pricingIndex.price("kycaid", "Non-document checks in government databases") }}, while its document check alone is {{ pricingIndex.price("kycaid", "Document verification") }}.

### AML add-ons

Sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening is often a separate line. Veriff adds {{ pricingIndex.price("veriff", "PEP and sanctions add-on") }} for PEP and sanctions screening and {{ pricingIndex.price("veriff", "Ongoing monitoring add-on") }} for ongoing monitoring. Sumsub moves AML screening into its Compliance plan at {{ pricingIndex.price("sumsub", "Compliance, per verification") }}. At ComplyCube, extensive screening with adverse media costs {{ pricingIndex.price("complycube", "Extensive AML screening (with adverse media)") }}, against {{ pricingIndex.price("complycube", "Standard AML screening") }} for standard screening.

### Manual review

Automated decisions are the cheapest. When a person checks the cases the software cannot decide, the price goes up: iDenfy's ROI calculator lists 24/7 manual review at {{ pricingIndex.price("idenfy", "24/7 manual review add-on (ROI calculator)") | replace(", by plan", "") }}, depending on the plan, and Veriff adds human review from its Plus plan.

### Volume

List prices are for small and mid-size buyers. iDenfy says its rate drops to {{ pricingIndex.price("idenfy", "Enterprise, lowest stated rate at volume") }} at volume, and Onfido's UK public-sector listing shows document verification at {{ pricingIndex.price("onfido", "G-Cloud 14, document verification") }}. At higher volumes, vendors move you to a quoted plan: Veriff's Enterprise plan is {{ pricingIndex.price("veriff", "Enterprise") | replace("Quoted", "quoted") }}.

### Billing rules

Read what counts as a billable check. Persona and Sumsub bill per successful verification, Enigma charges {{ pricingIndex.price("enigma", "KYB searches with no match") | replace(" (billed per match, not per search)", "") }} for a KYB search that returns no match, and iDenfy sells approved-only billing as an add-on at {{ pricingIndex.price("idenfy", "Approved-only billing add-on (ROI calculator)") }}. Two vendors with the same unit price can send very different invoices if one charges for abandoned or rejected sessions.

## How we built the index

A price counts as official if the provider shows it on its own website without a login, a demo call or a sales form. Plan fees, monthly minimums, per-check rates and credit packs all count. A plan marked "custom" or "contact us" does not.

Each figure lives once, in that provider's record in our database, with its source and the month we checked it. The tables on this page, the pricing block on each review and our rankings read from the same record. The methodology behind the reviews is on our [methodology page](/methodology/).

We re-opened every official pricing page on 2 November 2026 and corrected the records where something had changed. One page, TRP's, blocked our fetcher that day, so its row still shows the October 2026 check. Third-party estimates carry their own check month.

We leave {{ pricingIndex.excluded.length }} records with official figures out of the main table. {% for e in pricingIndex.excluded %}{{ e.name }}: {{ e.reason }}. {% endfor %}Tax is excluded unless the vendor says otherwise; KYCAID and TRP both state that their prices exclude VAT.

Inclusion and order in this index do not depend on any commercial relationship. Our [disclosure](/methodology/#disclosure) explains which providers we have paid or affiliate links with.

## Limitations

List prices are a starting point. Larger buyers negotiate, and enterprise contracts often include volume tiers, setup fees and support charges that never appear on a pricing page.

The rows are not directly comparable. A Travel Rule message, a sanctions name search, a KYB report and a document check with liveness are different products, and a plan fee with an allowance cannot be lined up against a pure per-check price without knowing your volume. That is why the table shows each vendor's own wording instead of a single "price per check" column.

Pricing pages change without notice, and some only render prices with scripts, which can hide figures from automated checks. If a number here differs from what you see on the vendor's site, the vendor's site wins; tell us and we will re-check it.

We have not been billed by these vendors, so this index cannot tell you how each one counts retries, duplicates or rejected sessions in practice. For that, ask for a sample invoice during the trial. If you are comparing vendors for a bank, our [guide to the KYC market for banks](/guide/the-kyc-market-guide-for-banks/) covers the questions to ask beyond price, and the [identity verification ranking](/providers/identity-verification/) and [AML ranking](/providers/aml/) cover the providers that only quote on request.
