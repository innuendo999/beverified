Our verdict

Onfido’s strength is the identity check itself: document plus selfie verification with liveness, delivered through mature SDKs [4]. Its weakness for compliance buyers is scope — watchlist screening is a separate module and ongoing AML monitoring is not the core product [4].

Since April 2024 Onfido is owned by Entrust and is moving to the Entrust Identity Verification brand [1][2].
