What identity verification covers

Identity verification (IDV) confirms that a person is real and is who they claim to be, usually by checking an identity document and matching it to a live selfie. It is the first step of KYC onboarding.

eKYC is the wider remote onboarding process that IDV sits inside — it adds data checks, watchlist screening and record-keeping. Liveness detection is one component of IDV: proving the selfie comes from a present person, not a photo, mask or deepfake.
