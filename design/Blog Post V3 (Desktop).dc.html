The three pricing models

Most KYC providers sell in one of three ways: per-check pricing, a monthly plan with a bundle of checks, or an annual enterprise contract quoted by sales. The headline price only tells you part of the story — minimums, overage and what counts as a billable check matter as much.

What counts as a billable check? Ask whether retries, rejected documents and re-verifications are charged. On high-reject flows this can change your effective cost more than the headline price.

What drives an enterprise quote

Vendors that don’t publish a rate card price on monthly volume, the modules you add — document, biometric, liveness, watchlist screening — and the contract length.

Compare quotes on the same volume, modules and contract length — otherwise you’re comparing different products.
— Andrii Klymenko, BeVerified
