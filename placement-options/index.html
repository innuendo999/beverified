Frequently asked questions

Does paid placement affect my score?

No. Your score comes from the same 42-criteria rubric described in our Methodology and does not change based on spend.

What's the minimum commitment?

Featured Placement and the Editor's Choice homepage slot run on a quarterly minimum. Category Sponsorship runs on a two-quarter minimum.

Do I get analytics during the campaign?

Yes. Featured and higher tiers include a monthly report covering impressions, comparison-table clicks, and "Visit Website" referrals.

How is sponsored content disclosed to readers?

Every paid placement carries a visible "Sponsored" or "Featured Partner" badge with a tooltip linking to our disclosure policy. This is not optional or negotiable.

Can I cancel or change tiers mid-contract?

You can upgrade at any time. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing quarter.
